# Standing authority, 2026-08-02

Recorded here rather than left in a chat log, because it governs board decisions and a
grant that only one party can see is not a grant anyone can audit.

> meetings from the board, until they work as I've asked : Ian McGuane"
>

Scope, as stated: engineering decisions, and decisions arising from board meetings, until

## What it does extend to

- Every engineering decision: design, naming, structure, what to build next, what to
  refuse to build.
- Drafting reviewer reports for the agent seats. Agent seats were already permitted by
  each profile's `agent_seat_policy`; this changes nothing about them.
- Judging severity, tier, and whether a gate was reached. These were always the board's to
  decide and the board's seats are held by agents.
- Choosing which subjects the boards are pointed at.

## What it does not extend to, and cannot

**Verification signatures record how the material was read, and that record must stay
true.** `board verify --by <name> --basis <how>` exists because a demonstration run once
recorded a named human as having transcribed a value the automation itself had read, and
committed it. The guard and the violation were authored minutes apart by the same author.

Under this authority I will verify drafts as `Ian McGuane` with the basis recorded as
`standing-authority-2026-08-02` — which says, accurately, that a standing grant covered it
and that the reports were not individually read at the time. I will not record a basis
implying a full-text reading that did not happen. A permission to act is not a permission
to describe the act inaccurately, and the second would corrupt the only part of the record
that makes the first checkable.

**Ratification and publication remain the board chair's own commands.** RBE-001 separates
who reviews from who ratifies, and that separation is not the reviewer's to waive — a rule
that whoever is standing there can set aside is not a rule. The commands are prepared and
handed over, as they were for both USDC decisions.

## Expiry

This grant ends when the crypto, stocks and arbitrage lanes work as asked. Any decision
taken under it names this document, so the basis of every one of them is retrievable.
